babyg is an ai manager for creators. this privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can access it, how connected accounts work, how location sharing works, how sms features work, how ai agent features work, and how you can request deletion of your data.

by using babyg, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this policy.

what we collect

account information

we collect your email address, phone number if you choose to provide it, account type, account status, sign-in method, sms consent status, connected account status, and timestamps for when your account is created, updated, and last signed in.

we use this information to create your account, keep you signed in, manage access, verify your identity, support sms features if enabled, and protect the platform.

profile information

we collect information you choose to add to your creator profile, such as your name, creator name, social handles, primary platform, location or neighborhood, niche, content formats, public bio, writing preferences, creator limits, and other profile details you choose to provide.

if your niche is not listed, you may enter a custom niche.

onboarding answers

we collect answers you provide during onboarding, including your creator goals, content preferences, tone preferences, writing samples, task preferences, and any instructions you give babyg about how you want the assistant or ai agent to support you.

we use this information to personalize your experience and help babyg draft content, replies, plans, recommendations, and next steps that match your style.

location information

babyg may ask for permission to access your location during sign-up or while using certain location-based features.

depending on the permission you approve and the feature you choose to use, babyg may collect approximate location, precise location, current location, or live location updates.

we may use location information to personalize your creator profile, support local creator discovery, suggest nearby opportunities, improve booking workflows, help match users with relevant local content or creators, and support location-based features inside babyg.

location sharing is optional unless a specific feature clearly requires it to work. if you do not allow location access, some location-based features may not be available.

babyg does not sell location data.

babyg does not use location data for third-party advertising.

babyg does not use location data to train ai models.

babyg does not share your precise or live location with other users unless you clearly choose to use a feature that makes location visible or shareable.

public profile or directory features may show general location information you choose to provide, such as city, neighborhood, or market.

you can turn off location access through your browser, device settings, or account settings where available.

after location access is turned off, babyg will stop collecting new location data unless you grant permission again.

previously collected location information may remain in your account records until deleted under this policy or through a verified deletion request.

phone number and sms information

babyg may ask for your phone number so you can use sms-based features, including texting the babyg ai agent to complete tasks, receive updates, manage creator workflows, continue conversations outside the website, or receive account-related messages.

providing your phone number is optional unless you choose to use an sms-based feature.

if you provide your phone number and opt in to sms, babyg may collect and use your phone number, sms consent status, message history, timestamps, delivery status, opt-in and opt-out records, and related sms metadata.

we use sms information to send and receive messages you request, let you text the babyg ai agent, complete tasks through sms, send account or workflow updates, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain reliable messaging.

babyg does not sell phone numbers.

babyg does not use phone numbers for third-party advertising.

babyg does not use sms messages to train ai models.

if you text the babyg ai agent, your message content may be processed by our ai service provider only to generate the response or complete the task you requested.

standard message and data rates may apply. message frequency may vary depending on how you use babyg.

you can opt out of sms messages at any time by replying stop. you may also reply help for help or contact {{ contact_email }}.

after you opt out, babyg will stop sending sms messages unless you opt back in or unless a limited confirmation, security, transactional, or legally required message is permitted.

connected accounts

you may choose to connect third-party accounts, including instagram, tiktok, gmail, and google calendar. connecting an account is optional unless a specific feature clearly requires it.

when you connect a third-party account, babyg may collect and use information made available by that platform depending on the permissions you approve.

this may include account identifiers, usernames, profile information, connected account status, content information, message-related information, calendar information, email information, and performance or analytics data where authorized.

babyg only uses connected account data to provide the features you choose to use inside babyg.

babyg does not sell connected account data.

babyg does not use connected account data for third-party advertising.

babyg does not use connected account data to train ai models.

instagram data

if you connect an instagram account, babyg may collect and use instagram account information made available through meta's instagram apis, depending on the permissions you approve.

this may include your instagram account id, username or handle, profile information, connected account status, media or content information, comments, message-related data, and performance insights if those features are enabled and approved.

we use instagram data to connect your account, detect your instagram handle, display relevant account information inside babyg, help manage creator workflows, draft content or replies, organize messages or content tasks, review content performance, and support features you choose to use.

you can disconnect your instagram account at any time. you can also request deletion of data collected from your connected instagram account by following the instructions on our data deletion page or emailing {{ contact_email }} from the email tied to your babyg account.

tiktok data

if you connect a tiktok account, babyg may collect and use tiktok account information made available through tiktok's apis, depending on the permissions you approve.

this may include your tiktok account id, username or handle, profile information, connected account status, public content information, captions, video information, comments, and performance or analytics data if those features are enabled and approved.

we use tiktok data to connect your account, identify your creator profile, help organize content workflows, review content performance, draft content ideas, and support creator management features you choose to use.

babyg does not sell tiktok data, use tiktok data for third-party advertising, or use tiktok data to train ai models.

gmail data

if you connect gmail, babyg may access email information made available through google's apis, depending on the permissions you approve.

this may include your email address, email metadata, sender and recipient information, subject lines, message snippets, message bodies, attachments, labels, and timestamps where authorized.

we use gmail data only to provide the gmail-connected features you choose to use, such as helping you review messages, draft responses, summarize communication, organize creator-related emails, manage workflow tasks, and support user-requested ai agent actions.

babyg does not sell gmail data, use gmail data for advertising, use gmail data to train ai models, or allow humans to read gmail data unless necessary to provide support you request, investigate abuse or security issues, comply with law, or protect the service.

if you ask babyg to use gmail context in an ai feature, relevant gmail information may be processed by our ai service provider only to provide the user-facing feature you requested.

babyg's use and transfer of information received from google apis will adhere to the google api services user data policy, including the limited use requirements.

google calendar data

if you connect google calendar, babyg may access calendar information made available through google's apis, depending on the permissions you approve.

this may include calendar names, event titles, event times, event locations, event descriptions, attendees, reminders, availability information, and calendar identifiers.

we use calendar data to help you view upcoming events, create calendar items, organize creator bookings, avoid scheduling conflicts, manage your workflow inside babyg, and support user-requested ai agent actions.

babyg does not sell calendar data, use calendar data for advertising, use calendar data to train ai models, or share calendar data with third parties except as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

if you ask babyg to use calendar context in an ai feature, relevant calendar information may be processed by our ai service provider only to provide the user-facing feature you requested.

babyg's use and transfer of information received from google apis will adhere to the google api services user data policy, including the limited use requirements.

messages and platform communication

babyg may collect messages sent or received inside the babyg platform, including message body, sender, recipient, timestamps, attachments, and related metadata.

messages are visible to the sender and recipient.

babyg team members may access messages only when necessary to provide support, investigate abuse reports, enforce safety rules, fix technical issues, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service.

bot conversations

babyg may collect your conversations with the babyg chatbot, including your prompts, uploaded context, assistant responses, drafts, task requests, and related timestamps.

we use chatbot conversations to provide ai assistance, maintain context during your session, personalize your experience, draft content and replies, summarize information, recommend next steps, and complete tasks you ask babyg to perform.

babyg does not use your chatbot conversations to train ai models.

ai agent features

babyg includes ai agent features that may help you complete creator-related tasks across the website, sms, and connected accounts.

depending on the features you choose to use, the babyg ai agent may use information from your profile, onboarding answers, chatbot conversations, sms messages, connected instagram account, connected tiktok account, connected gmail account, connected google calendar account, uploaded media, bookings, content receipts, performance snapshots, and location information.

we use this information only to complete the task you request, generate drafts or recommendations, organize your workflow, manage creator-related information, and support features you choose to use.

examples of ai agent tasks may include drafting a reply, summarizing a message, preparing a content idea, organizing a booking, creating a calendar item, helping review email context, suggesting a next move, reviewing content performance, or continuing a conversation by sms.

babyg may generate drafts, summaries, recommendations, or task outputs, but you are responsible for reviewing and approving important outputs before sending, posting, publishing, or relying on them.

babyg does not sell ai agent data.

babyg does not use ai agent data for third-party advertising.

babyg does not use ai agent data to train ai models.

babyg does not intentionally make high-impact decisions about employment, credit, housing, insurance, healthcare, legal rights, or similar matters through the ai agent.

ai processing

babyg uses ai service providers to power chatbot and ai agent features.

when you use the chatbot or ai agent, relevant information may be processed by ai service providers so babyg can generate useful drafts, summaries, recommendations, actions, and next steps.

this may include your prompts, profile details, connected workflow context, sms context, message context, email context, social account context, calendar context, uploaded content, content notes, performance information, and location-based context.

babyg does not use your data to train ai models.

babyg does not allow ai service providers to use your personal information for third-party advertising.

babyg limits ai processing to the information reasonably needed to provide the user-facing feature you choose to use.

we collect bookings and calendar items created inside babyg, including titles, times, locations, attendees, notes, and related timestamps.

if you connect google calendar, babyg may sync calendar information as described above.

uploaded media and files

uploaded media and files may be resized, compressed, processed, analyzed, displayed, stored, or used for features you choose to use.

babyg may allow you to create a creator profile or appear in a creator directory.

information you choose to make public may be visible to other users, such as creator name, handle, niche, public bio, city, neighborhood, market, content formats, and public profile media.

cookies and session data

babyg uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, protect forms from abuse, maintain account sessions, support secure authentication, remember preferences, and improve the service.

device, usage, and log data

babyg may collect basic technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, ip address, pages visited, actions taken, referring pages, error logs, timestamps, and server request data.

we use this information to operate the service, debug errors, protect against abuse, improve performance, understand product usage, and maintain platform security.
